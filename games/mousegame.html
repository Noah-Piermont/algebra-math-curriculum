Mouse Mansion 🧀

You are a homeless mouse in a house full of giant furniture. Steal snacks, hide from the human, and build the greatest mouse mansion on the block.

Move: WASD / arrows, or drag the left half of a touch screen.
New maps: the colored holes in the walls lead to the Kitchen, Attic and Backyard, where a cat prowls. Your black mouse hole is your home.
Look around: drag the right half (or click-drag, or Q/R).
Surprise: Space or E (15s cooldown, never in your safe zone).
Home: walk into your mouse hole, or press B. Missions: M
Hide under glowing green rings. If the human spots you, run into the yellow safe circle around your mouse hole. He can't enter it.
